## 2. What DLE OS is

**DLE OS** is an operating system for registering a digital legal-entity profile and running a programmatically managed business. At the core of one OS — **EVM** and **AI**. Data and operations stay with you; the profile and governance rules live in the EVM registry. For the owner, this is the first contour that restores ownership and control: not renting someone else’s cabinet, but your digital profile and rules that cannot be silently rolled back. In the near term, businesses get a new standard for company registration alongside LLC/JSC and other forms of ownership (*goal* after the regulator).

The template installs on your own infrastructure (including a local device **in the country of activity**). Perpetual license; the installed instance is your asset (see [LICENSE](../LICENSE)). Depersonalization, encryption, and personal-data storage follow the applicable regulator in your country of activity, including localization on your site (see [security.md — personal data](security.md#personal-data-regulatory-alignment)).

---

## 3. Why this is not science fiction

Countries already build **CBDCs** and **closed government blockchains**. The question is not whether the state will use blockchain — it already does. The question is that business remains a **passenger** in those contours: no own digital layer, only access to someone else’s system.  
Business moved into public networks faster than a shared legal bridge formed. The gap remains. Specialists and ready products are scarce — regulators look for **scalable EVM** solutions, not one-off demos.  
**DLE OS** is that contour: you have the OS and a digital legal-entity profile; markup uses **familiar identifiers and classifiers set by each country’s regulator**.  
Merging state and business contours, agreeing markup, and **protecting client interests** is the **vendor company’s** job: it represents clients and engages international law firms and major IT partners. The entrepreneur does not have to glue a stack and negotiate with the regulator alone — the path goes through the implementation program and pilot (§8).
